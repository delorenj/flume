# Research brief — employee company identity

Date: 2026-10-10. Operator delegated completion after the proposed research plan. This is planning, not authorization to provision live accounts or change DNS.

Decision: choose an idiomatic, inexpensive Cloudflare-first composition for employee contact addresses, authenticated access requests and truthful ticket/code authorship, independent of agent framework.

Hard gates: stable employment identity; no new per-mailbox subscription by default; no shared-human impersonation; existing Flume/Pilot/Krebs and 1Password authority boundaries; usable without Hermes. Preferences: Cloudflare infrastructure, fewer moving parts, open protocols and open source where operationally useful.

Method: three independent research dimensions, one bounded pass each, up to eight official pages per dimension (not a quota), normal verification. Public web-search wrapper and gh; three research assistants. Parent inspects local owner contracts separately. No source content or credentials sent to public search.

Dimensions:
1. Cloudflare inbound, subdomain rules, Worker storage/access, outbound restrictions, pricing and product availability.
2. Standards and framework-neutral open-source implementations: what establishes identity vs what brokers credentials; maturity/license and operational burden.
3. GitHub and Plane native principals, comment attribution, token scope/lifetime, account constraints and cost unknowns.

Deliverables: source digests, decision-grade research.md, requirements, architecture, one Flume epic with ordered stories, parent integration outcome and owner-scoped dependency requests. Account entitlements and installed edition behavior are explicit deployment readiness checks.

Existing requirements are context, not external evidence. Source claims must trace to retrieved documents; single-source, dated, snippet-only and account-unverified claims are labeled. Current official primary docs win over conflicting vendor-comparison articles. Work is isolated from unrelated main-checkout WIP.
